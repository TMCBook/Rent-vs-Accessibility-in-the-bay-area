How these numbers are built

Housing cost. Median asking rent of 73,708 active Zillow rental listings in each ZIP (all unit sizes; 1-bedroom shown separately). ZIPs with fewer than 8 rentals are grey. Map colors split ZIPs into seven equal-count groups.

Wages. Median posted hourly pay among hourly Indeed postings in the ZIP's main city (needs 20+ postings), otherwise the Bay Area median of $25.64/hr. Rent burden = median rent ÷ (wage × 2,080 hours ÷ 12). Spending over 30% of income on rent is the standard definition of cost-burdened.

Essentials nearby. Share of the ZIP's rental listings with a grocery store, a primary-care clinic and a pharmacy each within 1 mile, plus a bus stop or transit station within ¼ mile (about a 5-minute walk). Distances are straight-line from each listing to the nearest Google Maps place in that category. Walk Score and Transit Score come from Zillow.

Pattern. Each ZIP is compared with the median ZIP on rent ($2,995) and on essentials nearby (83%). "Affordable but underserved" = cheaper than the median, with fewer essentials nearby.

Service quality. Average Google rating of rated grocery stores, clinics and pharmacies in the ZIP. Review stars and owner reply rate come from the Reviews dataset where a ZIP has 10+ matched reviews.

Limits. Asking rents describe the market-rate listings on Zillow, not what current tenants pay. Places shows which services exist, not whether they're affordable. Straight-line distance understates real travel on hills and across freeways. Results are by ZIP; no addresses are shown.
